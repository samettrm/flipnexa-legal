FLIPNEXA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Publisher: BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş.

Summary

FLIPNEXA works entirely on your device. We do not run a server for the app, we do not collect personal data, and we cannot see the items, prices or photos you enter.

What stays on your device

  • Inventory items you create: names, purchase and selling prices, shipping, fees, other expenses, status and sale dates.
  • Photos you add to an item. The app copies the picture into its own private storage on your device. Photos are never uploaded.
  • A local launch counter used only to avoid asking for a store rating on first launch.

This information is stored in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photo library

FLIPNEXA asks for camera access only when you choose “Take Photo”, and uses the system photo picker when you choose “Choose from Library”. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking

FLIPNEXA has no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

Purchases

If you buy FLIPNEXA Pro, the payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details.

Ratings

The app may show the store's own rating window provided by Apple or Google. Any rating goes directly to the store.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item and its photo inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore deleted data.

Children

FLIPNEXA is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
