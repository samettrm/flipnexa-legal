FLIPNEXA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Publisher: BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş.

Summary

FLIPNEXA works on your device. Your items, prices and photos never leave it. The app sends short usage statistics without personal content (see below). Signing in with Apple or Google is optional; if you sign in, our server keeps only a sign-in ID so your account can be recognised and deleted. We do not see the items, prices or photos you enter.

What stays on your device

  • Inventory items you create: names, purchase and selling prices, shipping, fees, other expenses, status and sale dates.
  • Photos you add to an item. The app copies the picture into its own private storage on your device. Photos are never uploaded.
  • A local launch counter used only to avoid asking for a store rating on first launch.

This information is stored in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photo library

FLIPNEXA asks for camera access only when you choose “Take Photo”, and uses the system photo picker when you choose “Choose from Library”. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Optional sign-in with Apple or Google

You can use FLIPNEXA without an account. If you choose “Continue with Apple” or “Continue with Google”, Apple or Google confirms who you are and our server (run on Cloudflare) stores:

  • the sign-in provider and the account identifier it gives us for FLIPNEXA (for Apple, an app-specific ID);
  • for Sign in with Apple, an encrypted token that lets us revoke the sign-in when you delete your account;
  • the dates you signed in, and a session code that keeps you signed in on this device.

We do not store your name or email address, and we do not receive your password. We use this information only to sign you in and to delete your account when you ask.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings › Account › Delete Account. This removes your sign-in ID and sessions from our server right away and revokes Sign in with Apple with Apple. Items and photos on your device are not affected. Deleting your account does not cancel a FLIPNEXA Pro subscription; manage that in your App Store or Google Play account.

Usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and to find problems, FLIPNEXA sends short usage events to our own server (run on Cloudflare). No third-party analytics or advertising SDK is involved. An event says what happened — for example “app opened”, “calculation completed”, “item saved”, “item marked as sold”, “paywall shown”, “purchase started”, “the app hit an error” — together with:

  • a random installation ID created by the app (not your Apple or Google account ID, not an advertising ID);
  • the platform, operating system version, app version and build, and whether the device is a phone or a tablet;
  • the country of the network connection, which our hosting provider derives from the request (we do not store IP addresses or precise location);
  • if you are signed in, your FLIPNEXA account ID, so your own activity can be told apart from other people’s.

Events never contain item names, prices, photos, notes or any text you type. We use them only to count real users, see where people get stuck and keep the app working. They are not used for advertising and are not shared or sold. When you delete your account, the events are unlinked from it.

No advertising or tracking

FLIPNEXA shows no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Purchases

If you buy FLIPNEXA Pro, the payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details.

Ratings

The app may show the store's own rating window provided by Apple or Google. Any rating goes directly to the store.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item and its photo inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore deleted data.

Children

FLIPNEXA is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
