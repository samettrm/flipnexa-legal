Using the app

FLIPNEXA helps you calculate and record the profit of items you resell. You may use the app for personal and business purposes in line with these terms and the rules of the store you downloaded it from.

Your numbers

Every result is calculated from the amounts you enter, including marketplace fees, which the app never fills in for you. Marketplace fees, shipping rates and taxes change; please confirm them with the marketplace before you rely on a result.

FLIPNEXA Pro

The free version stores up to 10 inventory items. FLIPNEXA Pro is a yearly auto-renewing subscription that removes this limit while it is active. The price and billing period are shown by the App Store or Google Play before you confirm, and payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically for the same price and period unless you cancel it; on the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings or in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions; after cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. If Pro ends, every item you added stays on your device and can still be viewed and edited; adding more needs a free slot or an active subscription. Use “Restore Purchases” to restore an active subscription on a device signed in to the same store account. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
